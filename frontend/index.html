Riff Sketcher

Get the riff out of your head without an instrument. Tap it, draw it, tag it, then listen once.

    Time Pitch Selection: none

    1 Tap the rhythm

    Replay the riff in your head and tap Space or any letter key (or click the pad) on every note attack. Press Enter or click Stop when done. Record several passes for fast parts; they get merged. Then press → to draw the pitch shape.

      Edit the taps: Select notes on the timeline above; double-click empty space to insert one.

      2 Draw the pitch shape

      Drag points up or down to show rising, falling or repeated notes. The vertical unit is roughly a semitone. Points start at confidence 0.8; lower the confidence of notes you are unsure about, and the engine will lean on its musical prior for those instead of your drawing. Click a point to select it, Shift+click to extend a range, ↑/↓ nudge the selection, double-click empty space to add a note, Delete removes it. Hold P and drag to paint heights.

      3 Tag gestures and facts

      Select notes on the graph, then add what you know: how they're played, the shape of the line, or exact facts. Everything you tag constrains the reconstruction so it doesn't have to guess.

          4 Reconstruct candidates

          The engine proposes several plausible riffs that fit your taps, shape and tags. Nothing is played yet. Pick one to see it on the graph.

            5 Listen and refine

            Only press play when you have told the sketch everything you can. Hearing a wrong candidate can overwrite the riff in your head. After listening, click the blue candidate notes on the graph and say whether they were right, too high or too low; that becomes new constraints. Then go back to step 4 and reconstruct again.
